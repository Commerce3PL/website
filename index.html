FROM FIRST CONVERSATION TO DAILY OPERATIONS

A clear path from
inventory to customer.

Here’s how we plan your operation together.
The final scope, pricing, and timing are agreed before onboarding.

01

Tell us about your business

Share what you sell, where you sell it, your number of products, your current inventory, and your typical order volume. Tell us whether you need fulfillment, storage, space rental, or a combination.

Helpful to bring: product list, dimensions, estimated stock quantities, sales channels, and your target start date.

02

Build your service plan

We review your storage footprint, product handling, packaging preferences, and selling workflow. Together we define responsibilities, the order-sharing process, access requirements, and any additional warehouse support you need.

Before you commit: confirm the service scope, quote, availability, rental terms if applicable, and onboarding schedule.

03

Coordinate inventory arrival

Arrange receiving with the warehouse before sending products. Share the expected shipment contents and quantities so we can plan how inventory will be identified, checked, and placed in its assigned storage area.

Agree in advance: delivery timing, product labels, receiving instructions, and how discrepancies or damaged items will be handled.

04

Get ready to sell

For online orders, establish how order details reach the warehouse. Confirm how stock is tracked, how orders are identified, and who prepares shipping labels. Space renters can plan their own workflow within the agreed arrangement.

Before launch: confirm stock information, your platform’s requirements, and who is responsible for each task.

05

Pick, pack, and hand off

Under your fulfillment plan, orders move through product selection, packing, and shipping preparation. We agree on packaging materials, any branded inserts, shipping-label responsibilities, and the carrier handoff process.

Set expectations: confirm processing schedules, shipping services, tracking communication, and exception handling for your operation.

06

Review and plan ahead

Keep your operation aligned as inventory and sales change. Discuss replenishment, upcoming promotions, space requirements, and any adjustments to your service plan. Review a returns workflow if your business needs one.

Keep us in the loop: share expected volume changes and new products early so capacity and support can be reviewed.

BEFORE YOUR FIRST MOVE

A few things to know.

Questions about your specific operation?
Include them in your inquiry.

Where is the warehouse?

Our warehouse is in downtown Los Angeles, California. Request the street address, directions, and an appointment before planning a visit or sending inventory. Deliveries and visits should be coordinated in advance.

Can I rent warehouse space for my inventory?

Yes, we offer warehouse space for inventory storage. The right arrangement depends on availability, your storage footprint, equipment, access needs, and planned schedule. Confirm the included facilities and handling requirements before booking.

Can I store inventory without using fulfillment?

Tell us if you want storage space and intend to manage your own orders. We’ll discuss a rental arrangement and clarify which tasks you handle and which services, if any, you want Stow Flow 3PL to provide.

How does pricing work?

Request a quote based on your inventory, space requirements, order volume, packaging, and requested support. Confirm receiving, storage or rent, packing, materials, shipping charges, and any additional fees in your proposal. Rates and minimums are established in your agreement.

Which selling platforms can you work with?

Share your e-commerce platforms during the initial conversation. We’ll review the supported order-sharing process and any technical setup required. Platform compatibility and integrations must be confirmed for your account before onboarding.

What should I send for a liquidation partnership?

Include your company details, inventory categories, available quantities, condition descriptions, location, and pricing expectations. A manifest and photos help us evaluate the opportunity. Inspection, payment, and transportation terms are discussed before a deal is finalized.

How soon can I start?

Timing depends on space availability, the services you need, and the readiness of your inventory and order workflow. Share your target date when you inquire. Confirm your start date and receiving appointment before shipping products to the warehouse.
